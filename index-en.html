How to read the indicators

  • Criterion 1:all blocks in the day are checked. If fee evidence is missing, fees and ratios remain unavailable.
  • Criterion 2:coverage of the event index; a consistent index can still omit transactions.
  • Prices:historical daily references. Current spot prices are never applied retrospectively; assets without prices remain unvalued.
  • Short history:one point is an observation, not a trend. Missing days are not extrapolated.
