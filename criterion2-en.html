Incoming IBC — Receptions and success acknowledgements

Receptions are dated on the destination chain. Only ICS20 v1 packets with a standard success acknowledgement interpreted in the receive transaction contribute to confirmed amounts. Error acknowledgements are excluded; missing, asynchronous or uninterpreted acknowledgements remain unconfirmed. Missing confirmation is never replaced with zero. Denominations are reconstructed using the receiving channel, stripping the sender prefix for returns to the source chain.

These incoming series are neither net flows nor a transit measure. A success acknowledgement does not prove final credit to the ultimate recipient across all hops. Send and receive dates can differ. Gross series remain separate; net flows of successful transfers are shown below.

Confirmations and refunds — packet cohorts

Packets remain attached to their UTC send or receive date. Evidence from later transactions can update their status. Coverage shows the UTC boundary through which all required indexes have been searched without gaps from the cohort; a complete search does not guarantee all packets are resolved.

No-effect replays (NOOP) are excluded: source confirmation requires an executed message response. Recognised standard, IBC hooks and ICS29 acknowledgements are interpreted; others remain unknown. A confirmed timeout or error acknowledgement is insufficient to declare a refund: a matching bank credit with verified debit, amount, asset, recipient and application context is required. Refunds on a remote source chain remain unverified.

Gross series and receipts confirmed in the receive transaction retain their definitions. This reconciliation calculates no net flows and does not prove settlement to the final recipient across multiple hops.

Net flows of successful transfers

Net flows are calculated by local denomination: successful inflows minus successful outflows. Dates refer to receipts and sends, with the same indexed UTC day in both directions on all four chains. Evidence is limited to a common UTC cutoff; later evidence and incomplete scans are excluded from this calculation.

Confirmed errors and timeouts are excluded from successful transfers. Refunds are not added twice: this net flow is not a bank-balance change. Unresolved packets prevent a complete net flow for their asset; missing prices or units prevent valuation. Tokens are never grouped solely by their symbol.

USD subtotals use exactly the same resolved, valued assets in both directions for each chain. Coverage can differ between chains: these are neither complete totals nor a ranking. Total USD remains unavailable whenever an asset or packet is missing. Positive net flow means more successful inflows than successful outflows during the period; it proves neither capital accumulation nor transit centrality.
