Criterion 1 — Complete UTC days

A day includes every block timestamped from 00:00 UTC inclusive to the next midnight exclusive. The blocks before and after this interval are checked. A partial collection produces no daily total.

Ratios compare fees with supply states and issuance estimates over the same blocks. The interval differs by a few seconds from the 86,400-second civil day; its actual duration is shown. Estimated issuance sums the estimates from each batch, without extrapolating fees. Spot prices are not used as historical daily prices; daily USD valuations remain unavailable.

Criterion 1 — Earlier measured windows

Each row represents the specified blocks, never an extrapolated daily total. Duration runs from the previous block timestamp to the final block timestamp. The daily percentage measures time coverage, not a share of daily transactions or revenue.

Collected fees are evidenced by tx/fee or fee_pay/fee and tip_pay/tip events. For some failed transactions, an ante transfer to the historical fee module, corroborated by debit and credit events, evidences payment. Tips are counted once and shown separately. If evidence is missing, collected fees and ratios are unavailable. Estimated issuance applies the rate and supply observed at the start of the window to its duration on a 365-day basis; it is not measured gross issuance. Supply change is net and cannot isolate burns. Fees are never subtracted from dilution.

The displayed price is the timestamped spot price at collection. Any resulting valuation is a valuation at collection, not a historical window price. Supply and inflation snapshots are accepted only if the provider confirms the historical height.
